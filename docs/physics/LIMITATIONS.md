# LIMITATIONS & ALLOWED CLAIMS (`LIMITATIONS.md`)

This document defines the strict epistemic boundaries of the recirculating NFT lettuce digital twin specified in `PHYSICS_SPEC.md`: what physical and agronomic claims are scientifically supported ("Allowed Claims") versus what claims are invalid or unsupported ("Disallowed Claims").

---

## 1. Summary Matrix: Allowed vs. Disallowed Claims

| Domain | Allowed Claims (Supported by Physics Spec & Opened Evidence) | Disallowed Claims (Prohibited Without New Physics or Data) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. System & Hydraulic Scope** | - Simulating recirculating **Nutrient Film Technique (NFT)** gullies with shallow gravity-driven sheet flow ($h_{\text{ch}} \in [0.5, 25]\text{ mm}$, $S_0 \in [0.5\%, 5\%]$).<br>- Predicting how reservoir volume ($V_{\text{res}}$), gully dimensions ($N_{\text{ch}}, L_{\text{ch}}, W_{\text{ch}}$), and pump flow ($Q_{\text{pump}}$) scale water turnover, residence time, thermal NTU, and single-pass DO depletion. | - Claiming validity for **Deep Water Culture (DWC)**, **aeroponics**, **ebb-and-flow**, or **solid-substrate drip irrigation** (rockwool, coco coir, peat), where unsaturated porous-media transport and substrate CEC dominate.<br>- Resolving 3D turbulence, hydraulic jumps, or localized root-channel dead zones without CFD. |
| **2. Crop & Phenology Scope** | - Simulating vegetative **lettuce (*Lactuca sativa* L.)** growth from seedling transplant ($d_0 \ge 0\text{ d}$) to vegetative head harvest ($d \le 40\text{ d}$).<br>- Separating dry-matter carbon assimilation (`VH94b`) from osmotic fresh-weight water content (`AND05`). | - Extrapolating to fruiting crops (tomato, cucumber, strawberry), root crops, or C4 crops.<br>- Predicting reproductive **bolting** (flower stalk elongation), bitterness/lactucin accumulation, or **foliar tipburn** incidence (which is governed by localized meristem $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ transport under high daytime VPD rather than bulk solution $\text{Ca}^{2+}$). |
| **3. Water & Ion Mass Balance** | - Exact mass conservation of water ($V_{\text{tot}}$) and 10 dissolved ion species ($M_i$) under transpiration, evaporation, source-water refill, stock dosing, and physical leaks.<br>- Predicting bulk $\text{EC}_{25}$ and $\text{EC}_{\text{raw}}(T_w)$ from full ionic composition (`[T]` Kohlrausch-Onsager) and distinguishing transpiration-driven $\text{EC}$ concentration from leak-driven volume loss. | - Claiming unique inverse identification of all 10 individual ion concentrations ($C_i$) from a single bulk $\text{EC}$ sensor without ion-selective electrodes (ISEs) or lab assays (`GAP-05`).<br>- Predicting micronutrient ($\text{Mn, Zn, Cu, B, Mo}$) tissue toxicity or deficiency symptoms. |
| **4. pH, Alkalinity & Buffering** | - Predicting pH trajectories driven by $\text{NH}_4^+$ vs. $\text{NO}_3^-$ uptake (`ZH24`), carbonate ($\text{HCO}_3^-$) and phosphate ($\text{H}_2\text{PO}_4^-/\text{HPO}_4^{2-}$) buffer capacity, $\text{CO}_2$ degassing, and acid/base dosing.<br>- Predicting alkaline $\text{Fe}$ and calcium-phosphate precipitation above $\text{pH } 7.2$ (`ZH24`). | - Claiming exact speciation in concentrated stock tanks ($>1\text{ mol L}^{-1}$) or organic hydroponic solutions (bioponics/aquaponics) where microbial nitrification and dissolved organic acids dominate alkalinity. |
| **5. Dissolved Oxygen & Root Zone** | - Predicting equilibrium DO saturation $C_{\text{DO,sat}}(T_w, S, P_{\text{bar}})$ to $\pm 0.01\text{ mg L}^{-1}$ (`BK84`).<br>- Predicting longitudinal channel DO drawdown from inlet to outlet as a function of root biomass, water temperature, and flow rate (`CT94`), and predicting hypoxic yield suppression below $C_{\text{DO}} \approx 3\text{–}4\text{ mg L}^{-1}$ (`MG12`). | - Claiming calibrated site-specific aerator $k_L a_{\text{aer}}$ or channel re-aeration $k_{v,\text{ch}}$ from HydroGrowNet (`HGN25`), because `HGN25` contains **zero DO measurements** (`GAP-07`).<br>- Predicting root-rot pathogen (*Pythium dissotocum* / *P. aphanidermatum*) infection kinetics at high water temperature ($>28^\circ\text{C}$). |
| **6. Image-Derived Growth Proxies** | - Predicting 2D projected canopy cover $\text{CC}[\%] = 100(1 - e^{-k_{\text{ext}}\text{LAI}_{\text{eff}}})$ prior to canopy closure ($\text{LAI} < 2.0$, $r = 0.899$ with FW in `PET23`).<br>- Converting 3D RGB-D $\text{Area} \times \text{Height}$ to fresh weight ($r = 0.934$, $\text{MAE} = 23\text{ g}$ in `PET23`). | - Claiming accurate fresh-weight estimation from 2D top-view pixel coverage alone **after canopy closure** ($\text{CC} > 85\text{–}90\%$, where leaf overlap causes coverage saturation and correlation drops to $r = 0.539$, `PET23` Table A4).<br>- Claiming direct metric area ($\text{cm}^2$) extraction from `HGN25` raw `.png` masks without camera-to-channel distance calibration (`GAP-04`). |
| **7. Fault Diagnosis & Perturbations** | - Simulating the coupled physical signatures of pump degradation/stall, aeration failure, heat waves, probe drift, stock dosing errors, and reservoir/channel leaks.<br>- Separating a physical reservoir leak ($\dot{V}_{\text{res}} < 0, \dot{\text{EC}} \le 0$) from high evapotranspiration ($\dot{V}_{\text{res}} < 0, \dot{\text{EC}} > 0$). | - Claiming observability of faults whose physical signature lies purely in unmonitored states (e.g., detecting aeration loss on a farm that lacks a DO sensor before visual plant wilting occurs). |
