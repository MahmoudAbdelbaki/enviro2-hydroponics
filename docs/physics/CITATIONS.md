# CITATIONS REGISTER (`CITATIONS.md`)

Every reference evaluated for the configurable recirculating NFT lettuce digital twin physics specification is cataloged below with its unique `RefID`, full bibliographic citation, the specific modules and equations it supports, its exact verification access status (`OPENED`, `ABSTRACT ONLY`, or `NOT ACCESSED`), and source-level audit notes.

> **Strict Citation Rule Compliance:** Equations tagged `[S]` in `PHYSICS_SPEC.md` are taken **exclusively** from sources with status `OPENED`, citing the exact chapter, section, equation, or table number read. Equations associated with `ABSTRACT ONLY` or `NOT ACCESSED` sources are **never** reconstructed as `[S]`; they are derived from named textbook principles `[T]`, formulated as explicit assumptions `[A]` (with justification, plausible range, and one-at-a-time $\pm 20\%$ sensitivity rank), designated for dataset calibration `[F]`, or marked unknown `[U]`. All source material is paraphrased.

## 2. Critical Source-Level Audit Notes (Errors & Discrepancies Found in Opened Literature)

1. **Zhang et al. (2024) [`ZH24`, `OPENED`] — `mol/L` vs. `mmol/L` Printing Error in Table 1 and Text**:
   - In the abstract, body text, and Table 1 (p. 60), buffer and macro-ion concentrations are printed as `2 mol/L HCO3-`, `1 mol/L HCO3-`, `0.33 mol/L HPO4^2-`, `6 mol/L NO3-`, `0.33 mol/L NH4+`, `3.33 mol/L K+`, `1 mol/L Ca2+`, and `0.67 mol/L Mg2+`.
   - Cross-checking against Table 2 (p. 61, mass concentrations of salts in $\text{mg L}^{-1}$: $\text{KHCO}_3 = 200\text{ mg L}^{-1} \Rightarrow 2.00\text{ mmol L}^{-1}$; $\text{Ca(HCO}_3)_2 = 81\text{ mg L}^{-1} \Rightarrow 0.50\text{ mmol L}^{-1}\text{ Ca}^{2+}, 1.00\text{ mmol L}^{-1}\text{ HCO}_3^-$; $\text{Na}_2\text{HPO}_4 = 47\text{ mg L}^{-1} \Rightarrow 0.33\text{ mmol L}^{-1}$; $\text{KNO}_3 = 404\text{ mg L}^{-1} \Rightarrow 4.00\text{ mmol L}^{-1}$; $\text{Ca(NO}_3)_2\cdot 4\text{H}_2\text{O} = 236\text{ mg L}^{-1} \Rightarrow 1.00\text{ mmol L}^{-1}\text{ Ca}^{2+}, 2.00\text{ mmol L}^{-1}\text{ NO}_3^-$), Figure 4i y-axis (`HCO3- concentration / mmol·L^-1`), and the reported solution $\text{EC} = 1.0\text{–}1.2\text{ mS cm}^{-1}$ proves conclusively that every `mol/L` in Table 1 and the text is a **typesetting error for `mmol/L`**. All equations and parameters in `PHYSICS_SPEC.md` use the true $\text{mmol L}^{-1}$ units.
2. **Vanthoor (2011) [`VT11b`, `OPENED`] vs. Stanghellini (1987) [`ST87`, `OPENED`] — Daytime VPD Stomatal Resistance Cap**:
   - In `ST87` Section 3.5.2, Eq. (3.53) (p. 41), the daytime vapor pressure deficit modifier $f_4(e_0 - e_a)$ on internal stomatal resistance is explicitly capped at **$3.8$**: $f_4 = \min(3.8,\, 1 + 4.3[\Delta e_{\text{kPa}}]^2)$.
   - In `VT11b` Chapter 8 (p. 218), this cap is misquoted as **$5.8$**. `PHYSICS_SPEC.md` uses the primary source value $3.8$ from `ST87` Eq. (3.53).
3. **Chun & Takakura (1994) [`CT94`, `OPENED`] — Internal Unit/Scaling Discrepancy Between Eq. (4)/Table 2 and Eq. (5)/Table 3**:
   - In `CT94`, 4 lettuce plants were placed in a $15.3\text{ L}$ dark respiration chamber at $23^\circ\text{C}$. At $d = 26\text{ days}$, root volume per plant is $V_{\text{root}}(26) = 0.0698 \times 1.1230^{26} = 1.425\text{ mL plant}^{-1}$ (Eq. 1, total root volume $4 \times 1.425 = 5.70\text{ mL}$ in $15.3\text{ L}$).
   - The English abstract and Section 3 state that root respiration at saturation ($C_{\text{DO}} \in [190, 230]\text{ }\mu\text{mol L}^{-1} = 6.08\text{–}7.36\text{ mg L}^{-1}$) ranged from **$2.9\text{ to }6.7\text{ }\mu\text{mol O}_2\text{ h}^{-1}\text{ (mL root)}^{-1}$** ($0.093\text{ to }0.214\text{ mg O}_2\text{ h}^{-1}\text{ (mL root)}^{-1}$), which matches the Japanese abstract ($0.29\text{ to }0.67 \times 10^{-5}\text{ mol h}^{-1}\text{ (mL root)}^{-1}$).
   - However, substituting Table 2's regression slope $\beta_1 = -0.049\text{ mmol L}^{-1}\text{ h}^{-1}$ into Eq. (4) ($R = -(dC/dt)(V_{\text{sol}}/V_{\text{root}})$) yields a number $\sim 20\times$ larger because Table 2's $V_{\text{sol}}/V_{\text{root}}$ factor and $\text{mmol}$ vs. $\mu\text{mol}$ scaling were inconsistently normalized between single-plant and 4-plant chamber volumes. `PHYSICS_SPEC.md` documents this discrepancy and anchors specific root respiration to the verified abstract/text range $2.9\text{–}6.7\text{ }\mu\text{mol O}_2\text{ h}^{-1}\text{ (mL root)}^{-1}$ (and Van Henten 1994 root maintenance respiration $c_{\text{resp},r} = 1.16\times 10^{-7}\text{ s}^{-1}$).
4. **Andriolo et al. (2005) [`AND05`, `OPENED`] — Divergent Salinity Response of Fresh Mass vs. Dry Mass**:
   - While Ayers et al. (1951) [`AWB51`] reported a Maas-Hoffman yield threshold of $1.3\text{ dS m}^{-1}$ and $-13.0\%\text{ (dS m}^{-1})^{-1}$ slope in salinized soil, `AND05` showed that in recirculating NFT hydroponics ($\text{EC} \in [0.80, 4.72]\text{ dS m}^{-1}$), lettuce shoot **dry mass** increased up to $2.81\text{ dS m}^{-1}$ and remained flat to $4.72\text{ dS m}^{-1}$, whereas shoot **fresh mass** peaked at $\text{EC} = 1.96\text{–}2.00\text{ dS m}^{-1}$ and declined linearly above $2.0\text{ dS m}^{-1}$ at $-8.73\%\text{ (dS m}^{-1})^{-1}$ due to osmotic reduction of tissue water content (higher dry matter content $\alpha_{\text{DMC}}$). `PHYSICS_SPEC.md` explicitly separates the osmotic effect on dry-matter assimilation from the osmotic increase in dry-matter fraction $\alpha_{\text{DMC}}(\text{EC})$.
